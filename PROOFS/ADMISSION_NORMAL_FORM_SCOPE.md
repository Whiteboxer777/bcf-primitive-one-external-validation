
# Admission Normal Form Scope

This pass introduces a kernel-scoped Admission Normal Form (ANF).

It covers:
- normalized profile roots
- deterministic atom basis
- deterministic clause basis
- verdict-preserving DNF-literal compilation
- schema-valid ANF artifacts
- ANF/reference parity on the canonical kernel corpus

It does not claim:
- whole-family final IR closure
- general solver-backed scalability
- whole-family mechanized correctness
